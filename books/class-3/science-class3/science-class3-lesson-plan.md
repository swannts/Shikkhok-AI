## Mobile session pattern

1. দেখি ও প্রশ্ন করি: observe a source image or read a small checked text chunk; ask a question.
2. তুলনা করি ও ব্যাখ্যা করি: compare examples, distinguish observations from explanations and discuss the concept.
3. প্রয়োগ করি ও যাচাই করি: complete an activity and a starter check; explain the answer.

Activities here use source pictures and discussion. Suggested simulations still need to be built. No real heating, wiring, unknown-substance tasting, animal handling or direct Sun observation is assigned. Food lessons cover textbook concepts rather than personalised nutrition or medical advice.

- `chapters` preserves the 11 original chapter titles and page ranges.

- `sessions` contains 132 timed teaching plans, prerequisites and rubrics.

- Keep source page references attached to retrieval chunks. Do not invent textbook experiment results or specific facts.
- Let teachers unlock or skip topics. Allow text, touch or oral alternatives for learners.
- Distinguish seeing an experiment from performing one. Invite a learner to explain the evidence.

1. Transcribe or OCR and visually verify Bengali text, diagrams, units and scientific labels.
2. Create readable phone-sized screens and check each image crop.
3. Create and verify narration and accessible alternatives.
4. Add unseen assessment items and checked explanations, beyond the starter questions.
5. Build and validate any simulations. Separately review any real experiment instructions before adding them.
