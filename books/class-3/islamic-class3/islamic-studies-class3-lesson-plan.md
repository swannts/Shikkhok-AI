## Session design

Each lesson has three sessions: source-guided understanding, practice, and application with a starter check. Each session uses 1 minute of recall, 4 minutes of guided learning, 2 minutes of practice and 1 minute of checking. Recitation lessons require a teacher-approved model and teacher feedback; recognition questions do not assess recitation mastery.

## Goals, activities and starter checks

### islam3-c01-l01: মহান আল্লাহর পরিচয়
লক্ষ্য: মহান আল্লাহর পরিচয় সম্পর্কে উৎসের আলোকে একটি ধারণা বা উদাহরণ বলতে পারবে।
কাজ: পাঠের আলোকে সৃষ্টি সম্পর্কে দুটি উদাহরণ বলো।
প্রশ্ন: পাঠে স্রষ্টা হিসেবে কার পরিচয় দেওয়া হয়েছে?

উত্তর: মহান আল্লাহ

## App integration and publication

JSON includes chapters, lessons, timed sessions, source ranges, original starter questions with zero-based answer indices, and tutor rules. PDF paths are relative to the ZIP root. Source references always refer to the original book; split PDF page numbering restarts at 1.

Before publishing, verify Bengali and Arabic text against the source, have a qualified teacher check harakat and recitation audio, prepare phone-sized readable screens and accessible narration, and add checked unseen assessments. Full textbook exercises have not been transcribed. Every session is marked draft and missing text/audio assets are null.

The tutor should stay within the textbook, encourage respectful treatment of all religions and use gentle hints. It should refer questions beyond the textbook to a teacher.
