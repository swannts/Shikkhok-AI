## Mobile session pattern

1. দেখি ও বুঝি: source images or a short checked passage, followed by discussion.
2. অনুশীলন ও আলোচনা: the lesson-specific activity, with gentle hints.
3. প্রয়োগ ও যাচাই: apply the idea and answer the starter question.

Each session uses 1 minute of recall, 4 minutes of guided learning, 2 minutes of practice and 1 minute of checking. Teacher-guided chants or prayers need verified text and model audio. Open answers use flexible rubrics, not exact string matching.

### buddhist3-c01-l01: সিদ্ধার্থের চারি নিমিত্ত দর্শন
লক্ষ্য: শিক্ষার্থী সিদ্ধার্থের চারি নিমিত্ত দর্শন সম্পর্কে উৎসের আলোকে একটি ধারণা, ঘটনা বা উদাহরণ বলতে পারবে।
কাজ: উৎসচিত্রের চারটি দৃশ্য নিয়ে আলোচনা করো।
প্রশ্ন: চার নিমিত্তের একটি উদাহরণ বলো।
নমুনা উত্তর: জরা, ব্যাধি, মৃত্যু বা সন্ন্যাসী দর্শন।

## Import into the app

JSON includes `chapters`, `lessons`, `sessions`, `questions` and `tutor_policy`. IDs are stable within this package. Source ranges refer to the original PDF. Split paths are relative to the ZIP root. Suggested prerequisites are teacher-controlled; they are not compulsory access restrictions.

## Before publishing

Verify Bengali text, names, dates and any scriptural or prayer wording against the source. Prepare readable phone-sized crops, narration and accessible alternatives. Have a qualified teacher check chants and prayers before adding audio. Add checked unseen assessments and review responses beyond these starter questions. Do not infer recitation mastery from a recognition answer.

The Buddhist and Hindu sources are image-only. The Christian source contains legacy Bengali font encoding, so its raw extracted text is not a verified Unicode transcription. All sessions remain draft; null asset fields mean those assets are not yet created.

Teach religious narratives in their source tradition, encourage respect for all religions, and refer questions beyond the textbook to a teacher. Activities use pictures and hypothetical situations.
