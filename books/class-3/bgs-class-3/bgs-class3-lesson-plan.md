## Mobile session pattern

1. দেখি ও বুঝি: observe a source picture or read a short checked text chunk.
2. মিলাই ও আলোচনা করি: compare, match, identify causes or discuss a scenario.
3. নিজে বলি ও প্রয়োগ করি: explain or apply the idea, then complete a starter check.

Use labelled source maps for geography, event cards for history, respectful fictional scenarios for social topics, and picture identification for road and emergency topics. Do not shrink an entire textbook page into a phone screen. Narration, readable text chunks and cropped illustrations still need to be created.

### bgs3-c01-l01: প্রাকৃতিক ও সামাজিক পরিবেশের বৈচিত্র্য

## App import

- `chapters`: the 12 source chapters and page ranges.
- `lessons`: 36 numbered topics with goals, source references and split-PDF paths.
- `sessions`: 108 timed plans with suggested prerequisites, activities and rubrics.
- `questions`: 36 starter MCQs with answers and feedback; answer indices are zero-based.
- `supplementary_resources`: the shared word bank.
- Prerequisites propose a teaching order; teachers may unlock or skip lessons.
- Keep source page references attached to retrieval chunks and answer from the active lesson.
- Accept different valid examples in open responses. Do not require personal family or financial disclosures.
- Do not imply that a child must earn rights by fulfilling duties.
- Current population figures or administrative facts require separate verification when needed.
