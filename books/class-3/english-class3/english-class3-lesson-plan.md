## Mobile sessions

Conversation: listen and notice, practise a dialogue, speak independently.

Numbers: count and recognise, match and order, write and use.

Phonics: hear the contrast, blend and sort, listen and read. Audio must be verified before these sessions can be published.

Rhymes: listen and understand, words and rhythm, use the language.

Instructions: understand actions, order and follow, give instructions through a screen simulation.

Reading and stories: read and understand, vocabulary and language, retell and write.

Long readings can be broken into further screens within each session. Eight minutes is a design estimate, not a textbook requirement. Keep small readable chunks and offer pauses or extra sessions.

## Lesson-by-lesson practice and starter checks

All starter questions below are app-authored. They supplement, rather than reproduce or replace, the textbook exercises.

### en3-u01-l01: Hello
Goal: Greet someone and answer a name or wellbeing question.
Focus words: hello, morning, name, fine, thanks
Sessions: Listen and notice; Practise the dialogue; Speak independently
Independent task: Act out a four-turn greeting using a fictional name.
Starter question: Which is a greeting?
Choices: Hello! / A pencil. / Twenty.
Answer: Hello!

### en3-u01-l02: Talking about myself
Goal: Talk about favourite games, colours, flowers and subjects.
Focus words: favourite, game, colour, flower, subject
Sessions: Listen and notice; Practise the dialogue; Speak independently
Independent task: Say two things you like and ask the tutor about one favourite.
Starter question: What does favourite mean?
Choices: Least liked / Most liked / Very old
Answer: Most liked

## JSON structure and app use

- `units`: the eight original textbook units with ordered lesson IDs.
- `lessons`: the 41 original lessons with page ranges, focus words, goals, starter questions and split-PDF links.
- `sessions`: the 123 mobile session plans, each with an objective, prerequisites, four timed steps and an assessment rubric.
- `correct_option_index` is zero-based. Session exit checks refer to a lesson starter question ID.
- Sequential prerequisites are suggestions; teachers can unlock or skip lessons.
- One unit-end review is suggested after each unit, separate from the 123 sessions.
- Teach primarily in simple English. Bengali may clarify an instruction before repeating it in English.
- Offer captions and touch/typed alternatives to listening, speaking or movement tasks.
- The two lessons titled Following and giving instructions remain separate: Unit 3 Lesson 2 covers kite-making; Unit 3 Lesson 4 covers a thermometer sequence and library rules.
- Source quirks remain in the preserved PDF scans. App text and pronunciation require teacher checking; do not silently assume extracted text is flawless.

## Before publication

1. Check extracted English text against source pages and create short mobile text blocks and image crops.
2. Create and verify narration, especially vowel contrasts; choose a consistent pronunciation model.
3. Add source-specific exercises and checked answer keys beyond the included starter checks.
4. Review open responses with flexible rubrics and allow intelligible accent variation.
5. Keep the thermometer content as a screen-based reading exercise unless separately reviewed guidance is available for the actual device.

Audio, mobile text blocks and image crops are deliberately null or empty in the JSON. The course is marked draft.
