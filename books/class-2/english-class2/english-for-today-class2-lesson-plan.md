## Mobile session pattern

1. Understand a small source picture, passage or worked example.
2. Practise the topic activity with hints and discussion.
3. Apply the idea, answer a starter question and explain the response.

Each session uses 1 minute of recall, 4 minutes of guided learning, 2 minutes of practice and 1 minute of checking. Shorter sessions and teacher-controlled skipping are allowed. Listening and speaking lessons require checked audio or a teacher model. Text answers do not establish pronunciation accuracy.

## App import

JSON contains `chapters`, `lessons`, `sessions`, `questions`, `supplementary_resources` and `tutor_policy`. IDs are unique within the package. Keep original page references with source retrieval chunks. All split paths are relative to the ZIP root. Prerequisites are suggested, not compulsory locks.

Use fictional identities for language roleplays and give family alternatives. Accept Bengali or Western digits in maths. Ask one question at a time, provide a clue, and allow another attempt. Do not treat a single starter question as mastery.

## Before publishing

Verify Bengali/English text and mathematical notation against the source images. Prepare readable phone crops, narration and accessibility alternatives. Review model pronunciation audio and any scoring. Add new checked practice items and complete exercise answer keys if needed. Suggested activities and interactions are plans, not implemented app screens. All sessions remain draft; null assets have not been created.
